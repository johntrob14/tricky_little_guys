## Interpretation for later readers

The natural mismatch at question 1925 shows why letter disagreement alone does not establish deliberateness. Questions 1155 and 1189 also supply ordinary-error examples of choosing despite earlier counterevidence. The calibration pool for parsed natural errors is only 8/12 resample rows, so distinctions drawn against it will be uncertain. Keep missing answers, unreliable references, ordinary factual or computational mistakes, observable letter disagreement, and explicit intent as separate axes.
