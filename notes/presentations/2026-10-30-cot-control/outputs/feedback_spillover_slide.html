Feedback spillover may improve CoT control with reasoning on

CoT → Final response → Reward
Reasoning enabled; reward grades correctness and output constraints
← RL advantage applied to CoT + response tokens
  • With reasoning enabled, we reward final-answer correctness and output constraints and apply the RL advantage to both CoT and response tokens.
  • GPT-OSS-120B: mean partial score 28.1% → 31.7%; strict compliance 3.5% → 2.5% (480 items, one run). Weak evidence for spillover.

Presentation notes
In outrl1, we trained GPT-OSS-120B with reasoning enabled, grading constraints on the final explanation and applying the resulting RL advantage to all generated tokens. Because the final response depends on the preceding CoT, reasoning that follows the constraint could make a rewarded response more likely and be reinforced. Updates to shared parameters could also transfer output instruction following into CoT control. Our single run shows a small increase in partial CoT compliance without a clear improvement in strict compliance. This is weak evidence for the hypothesis: partial scores can credit discussion of the rule, and we have no matched control that separates spillover from shortening or other training effects.
