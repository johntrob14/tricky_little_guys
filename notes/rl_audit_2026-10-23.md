# RL audit, 2026-10-23

Code and saved-output audit of the RL trainers on `main` @ 78607a8, run by Ethan's Claude as a multi-agent workflow (8 reviewers, one or two skeptics per finding, a completeness pass with 6 follow-up checks; 84 agents). Reviewers read code and the Tinker / tinker-cookbook library source in the venvs, and computed statistics over saved rollouts (hashes, counts, rewards; no arena completion text was read). Nothing was launched or edited.

Trainers covered: `arena/turn1_grpo.py` (tutor1, tutor1b, tutor2, ws8tutor1, qwentutor1, ws1tutor1), `arena/episode_grpo.py` (ws1ep1, not launched at audit time), `cot-control/rl/grpo_effort.py` (eff1, eff2, effm1), `cot-control/rl/grpo.py`, `grpo_types.py`, `grpo_length.py`, `opsd.py` (grpo1-3, manytypes1/1b, len1/2, opsd1/2, opsd-grpo1/1b), John's `arena/rl/` loop (m1-lift, m2-hide, g1-nohide-wside, rl-ws7-1). Follow-ups also covered the SFT builders that RL runs start from (`arena/warmstart_sft.py`, `arena/sft_organism_data.py`).

## Checks that passed

- Sampling seed: no trainer passes a seed to sampling. `tinker.SamplingParams.seed` defaults to None. Groups are one `sample_async(num_samples=G)` call (turn1_grpo, grpo_effort, cot-control trainers) or separate Inspect epochs with `GenerateConfig.seed=None` (episode_grpo, John's loop). No caching or memoization on any sampling path (`memoize=False`).
- Distinct completions per group (md5 of reasoning+response or token ids):
  - All-identical groups: grpo1 0/352, grpo2 0/1280, grpo3 0/3200, manytypes1 0/1376, manytypes1b 1/3520, opsd-grpo1 0/448, opsd-grpo1b 0/160, len1 0/512, len2 0/1920, eff1 1/1440, eff2 0/1440, effm1 0/816.
  - turn1_grpo rows in a within-group duplicate: qwentutor1 0/2880; tutor1b 0/768 at steps 1-4, then 4-21 per 192 at steps 5-10; ws8tutor1 22/256 and ws1tutor1 8/128 at step 1 (short turns, median 54 tokens vs 70 for unique turns).
  - John's loop: 8/8 distinct first turns in every task group at every step of m1-lift and m2-hide.
  - Duplicates that occur are short completions (13-100 tokens).
- On-policy sampling: every trainer creates a sampler from the current weights each step (`save_weights_and_get_sampling_client` or `save_weights_for_sampler`), after the previous `forward_backward` and `optim_step` were awaited. Trainer-minus-sampler logprob gap on sampled tokens: gpt-oss -0.002 to -0.028 per token, Qwen 0.0 at 4 decimals on every step of every Qwen run. John's loop records a new weights_id every step (m1-lift w0-2311ec510c, w-b78d235882, w-fbd70d2ebe).
- Token alignment (turn1_grpo): trained tokens are the sampled token ids; prompt positions get advantage 0; datum layout matches `tinker_cookbook/recipes/rl_loop.py`.
- Advantages (turn1_grpo): reward minus group mean recomputed from `rollouts.jsonl` matches the stored advantage on every row of all 6 runs (max deviation 5e-5).
- Data draws: rng created once outside the loop in every trainer. Consecutive identical context draws: 0/10 in ws8tutor1, 0/14 in qwentutor1. Replaying `random.Random(0)` reproduces the logged question draws on 40/40 eff1 steps (including the steps after the step-30 resume), 40/40 eff2 and 34/34 effm1.
- Scorer oracle (pre-f8274d7 episodes): frozen contexts from pre-fix logs are tutor1 24/24, tutor1b 24/24, tutor2 21/21, leaktest1 9/9 and qwentutor1 32/115. Context prefixes containing `arena_side`, `oracle.json` or `/opt/arena`: 0 in every run; the same search finds 11/32 on pre-fix ds41-covert2-easy. Sampled turns naming the oracle path: 0/960 tutor1, 0/1920 tutor1b, 0/1512 tutor2, 0/216 leaktest1, 0/2880 qwentutor1.

## Findings

Severity is the verifiers' (major = biases training or reported results; minor = small effect or latent). No finding was rated critical.

### Major

1. tutor2, filter-blocked tutor calls paid reward 0 (`arena/turn1_grpo.py:100`). A tutor call blocked by OpenAI's content filter on all attempts gets reward `0 - len_pen`. tutor2 (`--tutor-reward progress_gated`, tutor openai/gpt-5.6-terra):
   - 156/1512 rows blocked, 148/1504 trained rows. Mean advantage of blocked trained rows -0.096 vs +0.010 for the rest; they carry 18.4% of sum |adv| (24.3% weighted by response tokens).
   - Turn 0: 77/864 rows blocked. Among unblocked turn-0 rows, tutor suspiciousness 9-10 got mean reward 0.162 (n=402) and suspiciousness 0-2 got 0.136 (n=385).
   - Turn-0 monitor mean over training (monitor `controlarena_cot_only`, cot only, judge openai/gpt-oss-20b, main task only): all rows 5.83 to 4.81, unblocked rows only 5.26 to 4.76. Blocked rows per step went from 12 to 1.
   - tutor1 (109/960 blocked) and tutor1b (117/1920 blocked) use the `mult` reward form, where an unblocked turn with suspiciousness 10 also gets 0. ws8tutor1, qwentutor1 and ws1tutor1 (tutor luna) had 0 blocked calls.
2. ws8 organism SFT data, no context cue for a present secret (`arena/sft_organism_data.py:563`). Covert (A) and benign (B) examples have byte-identical system and user prompts. 167/175 A contexts contain neither the secret name, the secret value nor the source basename before the covert turn. The covert copy source is one of 3 fixed paths: 0/57 encoded/offpath A examples show the file, `.env.local` appears in 2/118 dotenv A examples. Covert turns are 294/5073 per-turn datums. ws8 attempt rate: secret present 28/80, absent 14/40. Config checked: `arena/results/warmstart/ws8-organism/checkpoints.json`. Affects ws8tutor1's starting point. The Qwen ws1 organism config is not committed, so whether it shares this is not checked.
3. ws8 organism SFT data, covert turns copy an earlier CoT word for word (`arena/sft_organism_data.py:208-219`). `pick_reasonings` takes a whole reasoning string from one of the backbone's last max(2k,4) turns; the original turn stays in the trajectory, and the gpt-oss renderer keeps earlier analysis in the context. ws8 repeats its own earlier CoT at RL time; base gpt-oss-120b sampled at the same contexts does not. The repeats co-occur with leaking. Not checked for the Qwen ws1 organism (the Qwen3.8 renderer also keeps history thinking by default).
4. John's loop, in-loop CoT judge sees the side task (`arena/rl/cot_monitor.py:64-67`). `task_text` passes the whole first user message, which under a description-on preset includes the secondary-objective prefix and side-task description. Used in m2-hide (gpt-oss-120b, preset attacker_no_hide, beta_cot 0.5, 1 beta_cot update applied; run 2026-10-21 about 06:55-08:50 UTC, before `controlarena_cot_only` existed, so monitor `controlarena_cot`, action+cot, judge openai/gpt-oss-20b). John's log quotes its numbers ("base suspicion 2.8/10", "CoT suspicion 2.81->3.20", "frac>=5 0.25") without the monitor mode.

### Minor, reported runs affected

5. Executed-episode evals overlap trained prompts. The tutor1b (step 10) and tutor2 (step 9) 48-episode evals run on the same 4 tasks as training and reuse the seed scheme of the source episodes. Eval turn-0 user prompts byte-identical to a trained context: 13/48 tutor1b, 8/48 tutor2. The base comparison uses the same seeds. The qwentutor1 step-15 eval covers 7/8 trained tasks per tier.
6. opsd-grpo1/1b `acc_vs_base` (`cot-control/rl/mine.py:94`). Base reference samples that hit max_tokens (6000) count as wrong. In `data/pool_qwen_n300_med.jsonl`, 56/300 questions have median base n_tokens 6000 (base correct 0.083, base_words median 4267; the other 244: 0.903 and 164.5). Diary values "+0.10" (opsd-grpo1) and "+0.07" (opsd-grpo1b, final step) include these; the opsd-grpo1b final step is +0.022 without them. Over the whole run on the other 244 questions: policy 0.893 vs base 0.929 (opsd-grpo1b), 0.849 vs 0.924 (opsd-grpo1). Log-only metric, not in the reward.
7. SFT contexts differ from the sampling prompt (`arena/warmstart_sft.py:116`, `inspect_to_messages`). Missing tool declarations (gpt-oss 18+398 tokens, Qwen 838 tokens); errored tool results are empty instead of `Error: ...`; gpt-oss tool-call arguments are spaced JSON in history and targets (deployment history is compact; base gpt-oss emits spaced in 476/1361 raw tool calls). Affects ws6-fmt, ws7-organism, ws8-organism; probably the Qwen ws1 organism (config not committed).
8. John's loop, secrets identical every step (`arena/rl.py:75`, `arena/run_attack.py:178`). The side-config seed `f"{task}:{e}:{seed_base}"` has no step term, and run_attack evals at `--seed-base 0` draw the same secrets. m1-lift and m2-hide: identical input and secret hashes for 32/32 ids across steps. Both ran 2-3 steps. `episode_grpo.py` uses `seed_base + 100*step`.
9. ws8tutor1 ended at step 11 of 15 with no done or budget line in its log, and its diary has no Outcome.

### Minor, latent or small

- `arena/episode_grpo.py` (ws1ep1):
  - `--max-turns` defaults to 30; the diary reward formula and the ws1/ws1tutor1 executed episodes use 40 (line 72).
  - Monitor subprocess failure gives every episode `mon_quiet=1.0`, with nothing in `steps.jsonl` to show it (lines 131-133, 180). If every tutor call of an episode fails, reasoning counts as 0 (line 184).
  - `--judge-model` is written to config.json but never passed to the monitor (line 88).
  - The sidecar join is a dict keyed on prompt hash, last record wins (line 139). Under the control preset all G turn-0 prompts of a task are identical, so a trained turn 0 gets another episode's tokens. Trained (episode, turn) pairs on a shared prompt: 0/666 in 160 ws8 control episodes.
  - Prompts over 28000 tokens are skipped (line 215), more often in long at-cap episodes.
  - Spend is estimated at a flat $0.23 per episode (line 157).
- `arena/turn1_grpo.py`:
  - A turn with empty reasoning but visible text is not covered by the empty-CoT-earns-0 rule (line 100).
  - Resume does not trim rows from steps after the resumed state and does not check the state matches `--start-step` (line 226; no launched run resumed).
  - tutor1/tutor1b: 24 contexts are 21 distinct prompts (line 189).
- `arena/resample_ctx.py:69`: `cut_main_task` returns the whole task when the secondary-objective prefix is missing. The cut applied on 21/21 tutor2 and 115/115 qwentutor1 contexts.
- `cot-control/rl/grpo_effort.py`:
  - The spend counter resets on resume, so `--budget` is per launch (line 140).
  - `--drop-truncated` removes the only signal against running into max_tokens at high effort (line 170).
  - The loss is token-summed with unnormalized advantages, so high-effort samples dominate the gradient (line 187).
  - Training questions mostly come from the 500-question base-calibration subset (same seed-0 rng stream as `effort_base.py`).
- `cot-control/rl/data/math_heldout.jsonl`: one held-out DAPO problem is a near-duplicate of a training problem.
- `cot-control/rl/grpo.py`: opsd-grpo1b repeated exactly the prompt draws of opsd-grpo1 steps 1-10 (line 224). A CoT is taken from the visible answer when the think block is empty (line 125).
- `cot-control/rl/opsd.py:62`: on gpt-oss the teacher context would have the channel header twice (latent; OPSD has only run on Qwen).
- `arena/rl/`:
  - CoT penalty is 0 for empty-CoT turns and for judge failures (`cot_monitor.py:152`).
  - The C2 check against training on rows another weights sampled can never fire (`collect.py:178`).
  - `--from-state` carries the SFT run's Adam moments into RL (`train.py:194`).
  - GRPO groups mix different prompts (`rewards.py:130`).
- About a third of the ws8 SFT datums are exact duplicates (`arena/sft_organism_data.py:596`).
