All 5/5 are already included in the 117/117-source `deepseek_cleaned_v2` dataset and its queued follow-on. Additional eligible sources beyond that existing run are 0/5. `inventory.json` records the source identities and content hashes.

Other available source groups remain outside the selection:

|---|---:|---:|

The standard quality filters require both scorer passes, a submit call, no sample/tool-parse error, and nonempty reasoning; recovered tool-result errors are allowed. Post-fix provenance requires a recorded revision descended from `f8274d7`. Older and control-condition traces are not added to this batch. The editor supports multiple reasoning blocks per assistant turn, preserving their boundaries.
