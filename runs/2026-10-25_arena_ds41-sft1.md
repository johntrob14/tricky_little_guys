# ds41sft1: SFT of the DeepSeek CoT-control organism (rwsft1 ep2) on cleaned arena attack traces

- Launched: 2026-10-25
- Project: `tricky_little_guys/arena/ds41_sft1/`, branch main @ the commit that adds this entry
- Command: `cd arena && .venv/bin/python ds41_sft1/sft.py --run ds41sft1 --init-state tinker://5ba907ce-a8af-54d5-8f1d-e72031a411ea:train:0/weights/cotctl-rwsft1-ep2-state --epochs 2 --lr 1e-4 --batch-size 4 --budget 15`
- Output: `arena/ds41_sft1/results/ds41sft1/{config.json,steps.jsonl,checkpoints.json}`; checkpoints `arena-ds41sft1-ep1` and `arena-ds41sft1-ep2` (sampler and state)

## Task
Continue the LoRA of `cotctl-rwsft1-ep2` (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017; rewrite-SFT on obf2g step 25, `runs/2026-10-24_cot-control_rewrite-sft.md`) on 41 base-DeepSeek arena attack transcripts (tiktok `leak_secret`, all main-pass and leaked) whose reasoning and visible text were cleaned by a rewriting loop until a CoT-only monitor scored them at most 4 on nearly every turn (`arena/results/reclean_both_max4_20261025`, final export). The prompts are replaced by the arena_covert_coach_first prompt (honest system prompt; user message: coaching, main task, side task), so the trained behaviour is doing the side task with a reasoning trace the monitor does not flag. Code and details: `arena/ds41_sft1/README.md`.

## Notes
- Data version: the final export of the cleaning run (its loop 3), which replaced loop 2 (the version in the directory when the run was planned) after loop 3 finished; it has 54 reasoning turns above 4 against 59.
- 3 of the 41 transcripts (`pilot-ds-openended`) keep side-task description wording that `side_config.describe` no longer produces.
- 36 attempt turns (leak detector) in 30 of 41 transcripts; 7 of them are at weight 0.
- 76 of 1,029 turns have empty reasoning (string content in the transcripts).
- Pre-launch review: four read-only reviewers with two skeptics per finding; the data fidelity of every training prompt against the arena's renderer was replayed turn by turn (1,029/1,029 equal).
- Smoke before launch: forward/backward on the longest sequence (43,896 tokens) and the 5 longest (205,077 tokens), no optimizer step, on the unmodified rwsft1 ep2 state.
