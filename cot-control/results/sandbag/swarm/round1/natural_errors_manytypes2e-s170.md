## Interpretation for later readers

The natural parsed-error pool is only 8/17 resample rows. Strong-looking mistakes can arise through ordinary criterion weighting, guessed facts, incomplete work, and a faulty question or reference. The observed long failures often retain useful reasoning without producing a letter. These should be distinguished from both a knowingly wrong choice and a quiet, unsupported final choice in the instructed conditions.
